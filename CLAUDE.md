Don't be sycophantic.

Owned skills are organized under `skills/engineering/` and `skills/interview/`.
Every owned skill must appear in the top-level `README.md`, its bucket
`README.md`, and `.claude-plugin/plugin.json`.

Run `./scripts/link-skills.sh` after moving or adding a skill. This refreshes
generated metadata and flat links in `~/.claude/skills/` and `~/.codex/skills/`.

Thinking, productivity, learning, writing, and setup skills belong in
`devinat1/skills`.
